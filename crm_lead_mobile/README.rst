This module shows the *Mobile* field of the opportunity next to the phone, on
the opportunity form and on the kanban quick create form.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

#. Go to *CRM > Sales > My Pipeline*.
#. Create an opportunity with the kanban quick create or open one in the form.
#. Fill the *Mobile* field. When a contact is selected, Odoo fills it with the
   contact's mobile.
