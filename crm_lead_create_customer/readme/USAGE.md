1. Open an opportunity that has no customer, for instance one created by the
   website contact form.
2. Review the *Contact Name*, *Company Name*, *Job Position* and *Address*
   fields, which are now visible.
3. Click **Create Customer** in the status bar, next to *Won* and *Lost*.
4. In the wizard, choose between *Create a new customer* and *Link to an
   existing customer*. When a partner matches the opportunity email it is
   preselected.
5. When duplicate opportunities are detected, tick *Merge with the duplicate
   opportunities found* to merge them as well. The customer is created either
   way.

Once a customer is assigned, the fields and the button are hidden again, since
the values are then synchronized from the customer.

Leads are untouched: the standard *Convert to Opportunity* button in the lead
header already opens the same wizard.
